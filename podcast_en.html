The ITEA framework podcast

Deep-dive, explainer-style conversations on the algorithmic expropriation of labor: how agentic AI captures human expertise at the task level, what happens to professions and universities, and which mechanisms could compensate for it. Based on the reports and papers of Alberto García-Lluis Valencia.
